# 📚 Smart Study Planner

A web-based Smart Study Planner that helps students create a structured and effective study timetable based on their available time and subjects.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📅 Automatic Time Table Generation
- ⏰ Smart Time Allocation with Breaks
- 🧠 Study Strategies for Every Session
- 🔥 Focus & Productivity Tips
- 📊 Simple and Clean UI

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Enter your subjects (comma separated)
2. Enter total available study time (in hours)
3. The system:
   - Distributes time across subjects
   - Generates a proper schedule
   - Suggests study methods & tips

---

## 💡 Example

**Input:**
Subjects: Maths, Physics, Chemistry
Time: 6 hours

**Output:**
- 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM → Maths  
- 10:15 AM – 12:00 PM → Physics  
- 12:15 PM – 2:00 PM → Chemistry  

+ Study strategies & focus tips
